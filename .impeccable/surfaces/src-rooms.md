Scope: the screening flow — the dim clinical rooms reached from the landing. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: the concussed person, self-administering, alone, symptomatic while using it. No clinician present.

Job: complete (or deliberately abandon) a seven-task VOMS screening, rate symptom provocation honestly, and leave with a stage, a plan, and a printable summary.

Constraints that outrank expression: capped luminance and no pure white (photophobia); no motion outside the task stimulus itself (motion sensitivity is what is being measured); instructions persist rather than requiring recall; stopping is a first-class, always-visible action.

Direction: Three Rooms — Prepare, Screen, Findings. Each room is named, fills the screen, and holds exactly one job. The bright landing and the dim rooms are the same building; crossing between them is announced, explained, and reversible.

Memorable moment: the threshold. Entering the screening does not toggle a theme — it states, in the product's own voice, that it is dimming the room and stopping all motion, and why. The transition is the thesis.

Unresolved: whether the healthy-baseline capture is its own room or a labelled mode of Prepare.
